# Marketplace Card Standard

Marketplace cards present information in this order:

1. Media preview
2. Media type, Featured, and AI indicators
3. Available file formats
4. Collection
5. Asset title
6. Creator
7. Starting price
8. View and favorite activity

## Interaction standards

- The preview and title both link to the asset.
- Favorite controls remain independent buttons and include descriptive accessible labels.
- Video-containing assets display a play indicator; they do not autoplay.
- Hover motion is subtle and must not shift surrounding content.
- Keyboard focus must be visible.
- Missing prices use “Pricing coming soon” rather than an empty space.
